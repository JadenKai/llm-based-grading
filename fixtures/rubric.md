# Persuasive Essay Rubric (20 points)

| Criterion | Points | Full marks when… |
|---|---|---|
| **Thesis & Focus** | 5 | A clear, arguable thesis is stated early and every paragraph serves it. |
| **Evidence & Support** | 5 | Claims are backed by specific, accurate, relevant evidence. |
| **Organization** | 4 | Logical paragraph order, clear topic sentences and transitions. |
| **Counterargument** | 3 | Anticipates and rebuts a serious opposing view. |
| **Conventions** | 3 | Grammar, spelling and punctuation are essentially error-free. |

Deduct for off-topic content and factual inaccuracies.
